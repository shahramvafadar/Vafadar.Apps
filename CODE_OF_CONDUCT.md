# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this project's issues, discussions and pull requests is expected to:

* be respectful and considerate, regardless of background, nationality, language, identity or experience;
* keep discussions focused on the apps and the code;
* give and accept constructive feedback gracefully;
* never share other people's personal information.

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language and spam are not tolerated. Such content is removed, and repeated or
severe violations lead to being blocked from the repository.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private
reporting) or by e-mail once a contact address is published on vafadar.pro.
