Dastan – the guide of the brand

How the logo, the colours and the name of Dastan are used – on the site, in an app, on paper and beside the marks of others. It is made from the geometry of the logo by a script; where the two disagree, the geometry is right and this page is made again.

Owner of the brand: Shahram Vafadar. All rights reserved. The logo was chosen on 2026-11-21 (decision D-082) and this guide asked for on 2026-11-23 (decision D-087). The mark is not registered.

1. The logo

The logo is a symbol and a wordmark. The symbol is an open capital D with a dot in its lower left opening and a thin page inside its bowl: a letter, a point to begin at, and something to read. The wordmark is the name “Dastan”. It is artwork – outlines, not a font – and is never set in type.

Both are flat shapes in one colour each. There is no gradient, no shadow, no outline and no ground that belongs to the logo.

The wordmark never stands without the symbol. A square shows the symbol alone – never the whole logo made small.

2. The versions and when to use each

There are three colourings and no others: indigo with ink, all white, all black. The symbol alone has the same three: indigo, white, black.

3. Clear space

Around the logo, on every side, at least the diameter of its dot stays free – 58 units of the 346 the symbol is high, a sixth of its height. Nothing stands inside it: no text, no edge of a page, no other mark, no edge of a picture. More is better.

The symbol alone has the same clear space, measured by the same dot.

6. Type

The wordmark is not type and no font is named for it. Beside the logo, Dastan sets its texts as the site does:

  • Latin script: the font of the reader's system – Segoe UI Variable, San Francisco, Roboto – on a screen. No Latin font is part of the brand yet; for print, a quiet humanist sans-serif of the same kind is used until one is chosen.
  • Persian and Arabic script: Vazirmatn, without Latin digits (the “NL” cut), under the SIL Open Font License. It comes with the site.

Never set the name in a font to stand in for the wordmark, and never place a line of text so close to the logo that it reads as part of it.

8. Right to left

The logo is the same on a page written from right to left: the D and the wordmark are Latin, and a drawing is not mirrored. It moves to where a page begins – the right-hand corner – and keeps its own order, the symbol before the wordmark.

9. Icons

An icon is made by the script, not by hand: its sizes and its grounds are checked when it is made. Do not put the wordmark into a square, and do not draw the symbol in another colour to make an icon “fit” a theme.

10. What is not done

And never: redraw the logo; trace it from a picture; cut it out of a screenshot; animate its parts apart from each other; use it as a letter in a word; put it into the logo of somebody else.

11. Beside the marks of others

  • Each mark keeps its own clear space; between Dastan and another mark at least twice the diameter of the dot stays free.
  • The marks stand on one line, at the same optical height, with a thin upright line or nothing between them. Dastan is not made part of another mark and no other mark is put into the D.
  • Who shows the logo to say that something is by, with or about Dastan uses the files of this folder unchanged, and asks the owner first.

13. What this guide does not settle

  • The colours on paper: the CMYK values are untried. No spot colour is chosen.
  • A Latin font of the brand, and with it stationery, a business card, a presentation.
  • A voice for texts: the site has its own rules for how it speaks, in its documents.
  • The registration of the mark, and what others may do with it without asking.
