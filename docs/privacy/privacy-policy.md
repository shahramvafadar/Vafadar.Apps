This policy covers the apps published by Shahram Vafadar ("I", "me") under the name *Vafadar*, currently:

* **Finance** (`pro.vafadar.finance`)

## Summary

* Your data is stored **on your device**.
* I do **not** operate servers that receive your data, and I cannot see it.
* If you turn on backup, your data is copied to **your own** Google Drive or OneDrive account, into a folder only
  the app can access. You can protect backups with a password; without the password nobody, including me, can read
  them.
* The apps contain **no advertising and no analytics or tracking**.

## Data the apps store

Each app stores the information you enter (for example, in Finance: accounts, transactions, categories and notes) in
a database on your device, together with your settings (language, calendar, backup preferences). This data does not
leave your device unless you use backup or export.

## Backups

If you enable backup to **Google Drive**, the app uses Google's permission to access only its own hidden app data
folder in your Drive. If you enable backup to **Microsoft OneDrive**, the app uses Microsoft's permission to access
only its own folder (`Apps/<app name>`) in your OneDrive.

* Sign-in happens directly with Google or Microsoft; the app never sees your password.
* Access tokens are stored securely on your device and are removed when you sign out.
* Backups are stored under the terms of your Google or Microsoft account. You can delete them at any time in the
  app, or in your Drive / OneDrive settings.
* If you set a backup password, backups are encrypted on your device (AES-256) before upload. The password is not
  stored; if you forget it, the backup cannot be recovered.

## Export

You can export a backup file and share it wherever you choose. Where it goes is your decision.

## Purchases and donations

Where an app offers a "Pro" upgrade or a way to support the developer, payment is processed entirely by Google Play
or the Apple App Store. I receive no payment card details; the store may tell the app that a purchase exists so that
the purchased features can be unlocked.

## Android system backup

Android may include app data in the device backup to your Google account (Android Auto Backup), depending on your
device settings. This is operated by Google under your Google account terms.

## Permissions

The apps request only the permissions they need, for example internet access (for backup to cloud storage). Each
app's store listing shows the permissions it uses.

## Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Your rights

Because I do not collect your personal data, there is nothing I hold that I could show, correct or delete. All data
is under your control on your device and in your own cloud account. Uninstalling an app deletes its on-device data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new effective date. Significant
changes are mentioned in the app's release notes.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: _privacy@vafadar.pro_ (to be set up), or open an issue at the project's GitHub repository.
