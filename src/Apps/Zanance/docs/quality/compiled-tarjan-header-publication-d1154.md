## The marker alone is insufficient

The original faulting path masks the loaded header with~7 before its gc_bits
access. For every64bit word H, disjoint masks give:

`((H | 7) & ~7) == (H & ~7)`

Thirty-two new abstract checks enumerate all eight low patterns under four public
constant classes and preserve this equality. The universal result follows from
the mask algebra, not from sampling real headers. These are static reader/algebra
checks, not product tests or observations of the approved-but-unknown RAX value.

For an unchanged object/header source, setting just those three bits therefore
cannot itself change that masked base. A different forwarding path, an earlier
object's processing, another writer or a concurrent interpretation could still
matter. No such actual path/value is inferred; the public proposal is neither
accepted nor disproved as an indirect repair. In particular, the present unknown
classification must not be replaced with an invented marker pattern.
