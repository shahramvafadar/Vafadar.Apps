## Why the candidate was considered and rejected

The current complete row getter delegates dates to Microsoft.Data.Sqlite10.0.12. Its
[actual provider implementation](https://github.com/dotnet/efcore/blob/v10.0.12/src/Microsoft.Data.Sqlite.Core/SqliteValueReader.cs)
parses invariant text and retains a separate numeric Julian-date path. The temporary candidate accepts
only ten-byte ASCII yyyy-MM-dd, validates through BCL Gregorian month lengths and DateOnly construction,
and otherwise delegates to the unchanged provider. Every required/optional field remains complete.
The candidate is archived only as local rejected evidence, not a maintained build input.
