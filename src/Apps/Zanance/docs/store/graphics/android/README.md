# Android Play graphics drafts

The [Play icon](play-icon.png) exports the approved Zanance symbol and neutral
background from the [existing square raster](../../../../../../../branding/zanance/platform-candidates/ios/zanance-appicon-light-1024.png).
It is a full-square 512 × 512 RGBA PNG (57,377 bytes). Play applies its own mask.

| English | Persian | German | Spanish | French | Italian |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [en](feature-en.png) | [fa](feature-fa.png) | [de](feature-de.png) | [es](feature-es.png) | [fr](feature-fr.png) | [it](feature-it.png) |

Each 1024 × 500 RGB PNG pairs the approved Urbanist wordmark with the matching
language's Home and Reports captures from the [Android screenshot set](../../screenshots/android/README.md).
The promotional layout scales the full captures and does not alter the existing
screenshot files. The amounts and account names in the previews are fictitious.
The app name stays unchanged across the six languages.

All seven files were visually reviewed and checked against the relevant PNG
dimensions and colour types in [Play's preview asset guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151).
The feature compositions are release drafts pending owner review and selection in Play Console.
