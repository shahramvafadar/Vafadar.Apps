# Persian names of the collections

The forms in which the Persian translations of the histories of Iran, Rome, China, Egypt, Mesopotamia, Anatolia, Greece and India and of the Shahnameh write
names, gathered from the collections themselves (review D3 of `content-plan-histories.md`, 2026-11-28). A new
translation takes its names from here; where a name is not yet here, it follows the rules below and is added.

## Rules

* **Iran**: the forms of Persian books of history – اردشیر, شاپور, بهرام, خسرو, هرمز, یزدگرد; the Achaemenid
  kings as Persian history names them (کوروش, داریوش, خشایارشا); names of the Islamic period in their Persian
  form (یعقوب لیث, عضدالدوله, محمود غزنوی).
* **Rome**: Latin and Greek names as Persian writing on Rome renders them – the state روم, the city رم; emperors
  by their Persian forms (آگوستوس, کنستانتین, ژوستینین, هراکلیوس), numbers written out (دوم, سوم).
* **China**: pinyin read aloud – zh and j as ج, q and ch as چ, x as ش, z as ز, with the established forms ژو
  (Zhou) and ژنگ‌ژو (Zhengzhou); the parts of a temple name joined by a half-space (تای‌زونگ), the house after it
  (تای‌زونگ تانگ).
* **Egypt**: the forms Persian books on ancient Egypt use where they are settled (خوفو, خفرع, جوسر, رامسس,
  تحوتمس, آمنحوتپ, هتشپسوت, توت‌عنخ‌آمون, بطلمیوس, کلئوپاترا), otherwise the name read aloud with its parts joined
  by a half-space (سبک‌حتپ, حتپ‌سخموی), numbers written out (رامسس دوم); a Persian or Macedonian king keeps the
  form of the Iran collection (کمبوجیهٔ دوم, اسکندر سوم مقدونی).
* **Mesopotamia**: the forms of Persian histories of the Near East where settled (حمورابی, نبوکدنصر, آشوربانیپال,
  سناخریب, اسرحدون, شلمنصر, تیگلات‌پیلسر, نبونید, بابل, نینوا, اور, اوروک), otherwise the cuneiform name read aloud
  with its parts joined by a hyphen as in the English (اور-نمو, شمشی-ادد); a king shared with Iran keeps its form there.
* **Anatolia**: the forms Persian history uses for Lydia and Phrygia (لیدی, سارد, کرزوس, گوگس, میداس, فریگیه, گوردیون),
  the Hittite names read aloud (هتوسیلی, مورسیلی, شوپیلولیوما, تودخلیا), numbers written out.
* **Greece**: the forms of Persian books on Greek history and philosophy (آتن, اسپارت, سقراط, هرودوت, پریکلس,
  فیلیپ دوم مقدونی, ماراتن, ترموپیل, پلاته); other names read from the Greek (تمیستوکلس, لئونیداس, آنتیگونوس).
* **India**: the forms of Persian writing on India (آشوکا, چندرگوپت, گنگ, سند, پنجاب, گندهاره), Sanskrit names read
  aloud otherwise with the -gupta of the Gupta kings kept as گوپت (سمودرگوپت).
* **The Shahnameh**: the poem's own forms, as on Ganjoor – کی‌کاووس, کیخسرو, تهمورث, هفت‌خوان; a champion
  who shares a king's name keeps the poem's distinction (بهرام the champion, بهرام گور the king).
* A summary in Persian says no more than the English; a name in it is written as in its own entry.
* Sources are named as Persian writing names them where a form is settled (بریتانیکا, اسمیتسونین); a source
  without one keeps its Latin script – Livius, so that the website is not read as Livy the historian (تیتوس
  لیویوس), Asia for Educators, ROR – as do a museum number (BM 90920), a Latin title or word given beside its
  Persian (کارنامه (Res Gestae), senex) and Qin beside چین, which would otherwise read as China.
