## 2. Meaning and classical association

The name intentionally evokes two associations. The first is Persian **dāstān**, meaning a narrative or story. This supports a product that helps people follow the stories of people, places, organizations, fictional worlds, and other subjects. The linguistic association is supported by [Encyclopaedia Iranica, “Fiction i. Traditional Forms”](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/fiction/i-traditional/), checked 2026-11-15.

The classical association is **Dastān, a name of Zāl, Rostam’s father**. This is the accurate Shahnameh connection to use in the brand rationale; Dastān should not be described as Rostam’s own epithet. See [Encyclopaedia Iranica, “Zāl”](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/zal/), checked 2026-11-15.

These are distinct associations brought together by the plain Latin spelling. The brand should not claim that the two words have one established etymology. Its cultural resonance can enrich the identity while the product remains open to many cultures and subject areas. A concise public introduction should explain the experience first; the name’s background can appear in an About section.

## 3. Positioning and personality

**Dastan helps curious people discover how stories connect.** A visitor might begin with a striking photograph, a football club, a historical period, a company, a person, or a fictional character, then follow an intriguing connection.

The promise is:

> Start with something that interests you. See its story, follow its connections, and discover what comes next.

The primary audience is the curious general public. A concise first look, visual explanations, several coordinated views, and optional detail should make exploration inviting. Researchers and educators can inspect sources and deeper context when needed.

Image and video galleries can introduce public subjects and periods. Authorized family spaces can use the same core for memories and recent sharing. The identity should accommodate both without suggesting that private material is public. It must also remain suitable for culture, sport, nature, organizations, and fictional worlds.

The character should be curious, welcoming, clear, and trustworthy. The product should make uncertainty recognizable, let people distinguish interpretation from established information, and reward following useful connections. The word “story” describes an engaging path through material; it does not make historical claims interchangeable with fiction.

## 5. Logo and visual identity direction

The leading direction for exploration is **a few connected, flowing paths with one discoverable continuation**. It should suggest following a story and encountering a connection without becoming a dense network diagram. A subtle D-shaped composition is worth exploring if the symbol remains clear without explanation.

Two other directions can serve as comparisons: overlapping story windows, and a simple woven-path symbol. These are concepts for a later design round, not selected logos. A family tree, antique object, book, or clock alone would represent only part of the product’s scope.

Explore a deep blue or indigo foundation with a warm accent or teal. No palette is selected. The identity needs to work as a small PWA icon, in monochrome, on light and dark surfaces, and beside image-rich content. Latin and any eventual Persian wordmark should be evaluated separately. Content-status colors, including uncertainty or disputed claims, should remain distinguishable from decorative brand colors.

Optional language to test with the chosen name:

- **Dastan — Discover how stories connect.**
- **Dastan — Follow the thread.**
- **Dastan — One connection leads to another.**

The first is the clearest explanatory option. The second is compact and evocative. None is approved as the final tagline.

## 6. Remaining identity work

The next identity deliverable should compare three logo directions for **Dastan** in the same contexts: a PWA icon, a page header, and a visual discovery card. Evaluate legibility, recognition, product breadth, and differentiation from the observed namesakes.

Presentation decisions still include the pronunciation guide, Persian display spelling, subtitle, optional Vafadar endorsement, and eventual domain and store wording. These choices refine the selected name. They do not require another shortlist or block the bounded M0 implementation. Use a readable Dastan text wordmark and, where installation requires an icon, a simple original provisional icon. Record it as temporary rather than presenting it as an approved final identity. Do not copy an observed namesake's assets or visual treatment.

After a direction is selected, develop the symbol, wordmarks, monochrome variants, icon exports, palette, and concise usage rules. No logo generation, registration, purchase, or public branding change is part of this document update.
