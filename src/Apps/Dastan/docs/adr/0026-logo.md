# 0026. The logo: one geometry, drawn by the site itself

* Status: Accepted
* Date: 2026-11-21
* Decision of the owner: D-082

## Context

Until now the name of the site was set in the Latin font of the page, and the icon was an original
provisional drawing ([0004](0004-design-tokens-and-fonts.md), [0007](0007-installable-app.md)). Phase 20 of the
delivery plan said "not in this phase: a logo". On 2026-11-21 the owner chose a logo and handed over a picture
of it: a symbol – an open capital D, a dot in its lower left opening, a thin page inside its bowl – and the
wordmark "Dastan". The picture is pixels; no vector file and no name of a font came with it.

## Decision

1. **The picture is reconstructed, not redesigned.** The large logo of the picture is the reference. Its
   outlines are traced and fitted with curves once; the result – `branding/dastan/source/dastan-geometry.json` –
   is from then on the one source of the geometry. The wordmark is artwork: outlines, no font. The colours are
   the flat colours of the brand, indigo `#3B3A8F` and ink `#1C1B2E`, which are the accent and the text of the
   light appearance of the site already.
2. **Every asset is made from that geometry by one script** (`branding/dastan/scripts/build.mjs`): the masters,
   the variants in colour, white and black, the icons, their PNG and ICO files, and a sheet that shows the
   reference, the master and the two over each other. PNGs are drawn by the browser of the test container; the
   script installs nothing. The folder follows `branding/zanance`.
3. **The site draws the logo itself.** The script writes the geometry into `Site/BrandGeometry.cs`;
   `Logo.razor` puts it into the head of every page as a drawing in the page. So the logo costs no request,
   takes the colours of the appearance the visitor chose – indigo and ink on a light ground, white on a dark
   one –, and the site gains no dependency. The picture of a shared link (`PreviewPicture`) draws the same
   geometry in white on indigo.
4. **Named once.** The link of the head is named "Dastan" (`aria-label`); the drawing in it is hidden from a
   screen reader. A title of a page still says "Dastan" in words.
5. **Never mirrored.** The D and the wordmark are Latin. A drawing has no direction of writing, and nothing
   turns it on a Persian page.
6. **Icons.** A square icon is the symbol alone, white on indigo. `favicon.svg` and `favicon.ico` (16, 32 and
   48 pixels) for a tab; `icon-192` and `icon-512` with round corners for the manifest, purpose `any`;
   `icon-maskable-192` and `icon-maskable-512`, a whole opaque square with the whole symbol inside the circle
   of 40 %, purpose `maskable`; `apple-touch-icon` of 180 pixels, a whole opaque square. At 16 and 32 pixels
   the icon is the small-size variant, in which the page and the dot are heavier – named as such and shown
   beside the master in the sheet.
7. **What a device kept.** The worker keeps the icon with the page for "no connection"; its store is named anew
   (`dastan-shell-2`), which makes a browser drop the earlier store with the earlier icon. The store of the
   pages a visitor read (`dastan-read-1`) is not touched: nothing a visitor kept is lost. The name, the
   identity, the start, the scope and the colours of the manifest are as they were.

8. **The tool of the editors** (added on 2026-11-21) shows the symbol alone before its name. Its name, "Dastan
   Studio", is written in words in the language of the editor, so the wordmark – which says "Dastan" and is
   Latin – does not stand beside it, and the drawing is silent. The tool does not refer to the site: the script
   writes the same geometry into a `BrandGeometry.cs` of its own and places the icon of a tab in its files.
   The tool is no app to install: it has no manifest and no other icon. Somebody who is not signed in gets the
   icon too – a browser asks for it before anybody signs in.

## Consequences

* A change of the logo is a change of the geometry and one run of the script; `LogoTests` fail when the site
  and the folder of the brand say different things.
* The theme colour of the manifest stays the darker indigo of the head of the browser (`#2f2e7a`); the icon is
  the indigo of the brand. They are two colours on purpose of ADR 0004, not a mistake of this one.
* `icon.svg` and `icons/icon-maskable.svg` are gone; a link somebody kept to `/icon.svg` is not found.

## What was checked, and what was not

Checked: the master against the reference, side by side and one over the other (2.6 % of the pixels with ink
differ, the soft edges of the picture); every variant drawn; the symbol at 16, 20, 24, 32, 48 and 64 pixels and
the logo at 24 to 40 pixels, looked at; the maskable icon under a circle and a rounded square; the size and the
ground of every PNG; the head of the site in English and Persian, light and dark, at 360 and 1280 pixels, in
Chromium; the addresses of the icons and of the manifest; the head of the tool of the editors in English,
light, 1280 pixels and in Persian, dark, 360 pixels, on the machine of development.

Not checked: the app installed on a phone or a computer, and what a launcher makes of the maskable icon; the
icon of a tab in Safari and Firefox by eye; the logo printed; a screen reader. The original vector file of the
logo was not available, so the geometry is a reconstruction of a picture and not a recovery of a source.
