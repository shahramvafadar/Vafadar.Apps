# Watching people use Dastan – a script

Written with step 20.12 of the [delivery plan](delivery-plan.md). It is a script for the owner: five people,
four tasks, and what counts as done without help. **Watching them is the owner's; nothing of it has been done
yet.** The script changes nothing on the site and asks nobody for an account of somebody else.

## Who

Five people who have not seen the site. Between them: at least two who read Persian first and two who read
English first; at least two on a phone of their own and two on a computer; at least one who does not use a
mouse or uses a screen reader, if somebody like that can be asked. Nobody who worked on the site.

## Before

* The site as the owner runs it, with the collection that is shown to everybody, and accounts open (or an
  invitation for each person).
* One made-up account of the owner's that the person can ask for a relationship in task 3. No real data of
  somebody else is needed.
* Say once, in these words or their Persian: "I am trying the site, not you. Say aloud what you look for and
  what you expect. I will not help; if you are stuck, say so and we go on to the next task."
* Note the device, the browser, the language chosen, and the time each task took.

## The four tasks

Each is read to the person as written. Nothing is pointed at.

1. **Find and follow.** "You have heard the name Rakhsh and do not know who or what it is. Find out, find out
   who it belongs to, and come back to where you began."
   *Done without help when:* the person reaches the page of Rakhsh, names Rostam as the one it belongs to from
   something they read on the site, and is back at the start – all without typing an address.
2. **Read without being told the end.** "Read the story as far as its second part. Then look at who Rostam is
   connected with." Afterwards ask: "What do you know about how the story ends?"
   *Done without help when:* the person sets how far they have read, opens the connections of Rostam, and
   cannot say from the site what happens later – and can say where they would read on.
3. **Connect, and decide who sees it.** "Make an account. Ask the account called (the owner's made-up name) to
   be shown as your friend. When it is accepted, make sure nobody but the two of you can see it. Then say
   where you studied, so that former classmates could find you – without showing them the years."
   *Done without help when:* the request is made; after the owner accepts it the person finds where the
   audience of the relationship is set and leaves or sets it to themselves; a place is added, set to be found,
   and its dates are not shown.
4. **A family tree, shared and taken back.** "Begin a family tree with three people of a made-up family: two
   parents and a child. Let your friend see it. Then take that back."
   *Done without help when:* three records and two links of a parent exist, the friend is named as somebody
   who may see the tree, and is then not named any more – the person finding "change" and "share" on the page
   of the tree by themselves.

## What to write down

For each task and person: done without help, done with a hint (which), or not done; where they looked first;
the words they used for what they looked for; anything they took for something else (a record in a tree for an
account, a figure of a story for a person of history, a window of time for a moment); anything they pressed
that did nothing.

## What it is measured against

* A task counts as passed when four of the five people do it without help.
* Anybody taking something for something else in a way that would make them share more than they meant –
  showing a relationship, the dates of a place, a tree or a photograph to more people than intended – is a
  finding to correct before anything else, however many people passed.
* What is found goes into the delivery plan as findings with the page and the words of the person; nothing is
  changed while somebody is watched.
