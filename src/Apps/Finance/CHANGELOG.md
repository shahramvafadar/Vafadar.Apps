### Added

- App skeleton for Android, iOS and Windows: home and settings tabs.
- Language selection (English, Persian, German) with immediate switching and right-to-left layout for Persian.
- Calendar selection (Gregorian, Persian), independent of the language.
- On-device SQLite database with automatic migrations, registered for backups.
